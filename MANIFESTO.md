# Why Elbowpatch

We live among subscriptions and assistants. Every tool wants a monthly fee, and more and more of them offer to do the creative part for you: write the paragraph, finish the thought, polish the voice until it is no longer yours.

Elbowpatch goes the other way.

It is a quiet room for doing that part yourself. No one finishes your sentences. Nothing hurries you, measures you or asks you to upgrade. You sit down, the page is there, and the words that end up on it are the ones you found. That slow, uncertain, sometimes difficult work is not a problem to be automated. It is the point.

## What we believe

**Your creativity is yours to do.** The struggle to find the right word is where writing happens. We will not put a machine on the page to do it for you.

**Calm is a feature.** One column, good type, nothing else on screen. Everything else waits until you ask for it and leaves when you are done.

**Never lose a word.** Saving is not your job. Every moment is kept, and going back never throws anything away, so you can try, cut and rewrite without fear.

**Your words are yours.** They live on your device, in plain Markdown, and leave only for a place you connect yourself. You can take everything with you, in one click, at any time.

**No one is watching.** No account to start, no tracking inside the editor, no server that reads what you write. We count visits, never words.

**Speak like a person.** Drafts, moments, versions, bring this back. Never "commit", "merge" or "diff".

## Free, open, and staying that way

Elbowpatch is free, and it always will be. There is no subscription, no paid tier and no feature held back for money. It is open source under the MIT License, and it is a static site anyone can host, so it can never be taken away: if this project ever stopped, your writing and the tool would both still be yours.

It will keep growing, carefully. A feature belongs only if it makes the page quieter, the history more trustworthy, or your writing more your own.

## What we will not do

No AI on the page. No subscriptions, no accounts, no proprietary cloud. No streaks, goals or scores in the editor. No real-time collaboration. We are not trying to match the big apps feature for feature.

## Who it is for

First, the person building it, who writes in it every day. Then anyone who keeps a journal, works on essays, or is writing a book, and wants a calm place to do it on their own.
