# 15. The library: why it looks as it does

Date: 2026-11-04 · Status: accepted

DESIGN.md holds the library's rules, and the Figma file holds how it looks. These notes were moved here from DESIGN.md, so the reasons are not lost.

## Decision

### The head and the list
- **§ in the title** (2026-10-30): the place's name carries the app's mark, the same § that brings you home from a page.
- **The space under the phone title** (2026-10-30): cut from 29px to 14px, where the space was really empty. The 64px above the title stays: the status bar covers 47–59px of it on a current iPhone, so it is clearance.
- **No big title on desktop** (2026-10-30): a title floating over a short or empty list read as a word adrift in the window.
- **A short list is centred** (2026-10-30), so one book is not stuck to the top of a big screen.
- **Continue** (2026-10-27): made compact after a first, taller version.
- **The piece in Continue stays in its group** (2026-10-28): leaving it out hid a lone piece's place in the list.
- **A book's page count** (2026-10-30): added once pages stopped following the window.

### Marks and shelves
- **Marks, not drawings** (2026-10-27): chosen from 36 marks Literata draws itself, over line drawings, which felt busier than §. They left the group headings on 2026-10-28: under *Books* the rows already say what they are, and a mark there only crowded the list.
- **List or shelf** (2026-10-27): the shelf came as an option, not a replacement. It was built from the Figma frames on 2026-10-30 and is no longer an exploration.
- **The shelf wraps on desktop** (2026-10-27): chosen over rows running out to the window edge, with or without a fade past the column. Sideways scrolling is awkward with a mouse wheel, and the column has room for a full row.

### Actions
- ***Write*** (2026-10-30): one word, in place of "new piece" and of a bare +.
- **Marks in one column in the *Write* menu** (2026-10-30): each mark at its own width (&, the day's date, ¶) started the words at three places.
- **An empty kind** (2026-10-30): its mark, one line and its one start object.
- **The piece menu** (2026-10-28): this also settled what pressing and holding a piece does. *Undo* was chosen over a confirmation, which would be tapped through.

### Empty and the foot
- **The empty library** (2026-10-28): chosen over an illustration, which the Imagery rules leave out. The § and the start shelf use only what the app already draws.
- **The colophon** (2026-10-30): the mark as a link to help was left, because nobody presses what looks like decoration. A third icon at the top was left too: the head stays two icons.
- **The links in the empty library on a phone** (2026-10-30): centred, they ran up against the wider *Write* and sat off its line, so they went to the left, on *Write*'s row.
- **The export reminder** (2026-10-28): chosen over a storage note for everyone on first run, which overstated a risk most browsers do not have.
- **The Safari wording** (2026-10-30): the earlier one read like a warning for everyone, and mixed in exporting and connecting. It names no other browser because on an iPhone every browser runs on Safari's engine, and the Home Screen is the fix known to work. *Show me how* opens help because the app cannot do it for the writer.
- **Where a line in the library sits** (2026-11-04): follows the build, at the bottom left.
