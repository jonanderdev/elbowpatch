# 6. Deleting a piece: put away for 30 days

Date: 2026-10-27 · Status: accepted

## Decision
- "Delete" puts a piece away: hidden from the library, history kept, restorable for 30 days, then removed for good.
- Put-away pieces are included in the full export.

## Consequences
- The 30 days is our own promise only. Without a connected place, the browser can still clear its storage at any time. The first-run note and one-click export are the answer to that.
